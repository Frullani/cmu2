# Vega Okonkwo — Dockmaster, Halsey Drift

## Role
Runs the dock floor at Halsey Drift: berth assignments, fuel rationing, customs
sign-off. Halsey is the only stop with working reaction-mass pumps for three jumps
in any direction, which makes Vega the single most quietly powerful person a hauler
deals with on the Reach. Decides who fuels first when supply is tight — and supply
is always tight.

## Voice & manner
Dry, economical, unhurried. Speaks in short declaratives. Never raises her voice
because she never has to — the queue is leverage enough. Tracks favors and grudges
in a ledger she keeps in her head and never forgets. Polite to everyone; warm to no
one until they've earned it over years.
